# Multimedia & On-Device AI — week of 28 September to 4 October 2026

*Two Chinese phone makers shipped flagships this week that pair their primary application processor with a separate, self-branded imaging chip — Honor's new HONOR Imaging Chip H1 behind an ARRI-co-engineered cinema pipeline, and vivo's Imaging Chip V3+ behind a MediaTek co-developed Dimensity 9500 Super Edition.*

## Top items

### Honor pairs a self-developed imaging chip with an ARRI-engineered cinema pipeline on the Magic9 series
**Honor** · camera/imaging silicon · 28 September 2026 · Shipped (China)

Honor launched the Magic9 series, with the Magic9 Pro Max built on a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 platform alongside a separate, newly named "HONOR Imaging Chip H1," which Honor says is built on a 6nm process and delivers "18.8 TOPS/W energy efficiency." The imaging pipeline was co-engineered with ARRI, incorporating "selected elements of ARRI Image Science," the ARRI LogC3 curve, 10-bit colour depth, ARRI Wide Gamut 3, 14 preset "ARRI Looks" and an 87-grading "ARRI Look Library." The main and telephoto cameras are both 200MP (1/1.28-inch f/1.57 main, 1/1.4-inch f/2.6 telephoto), and Honor states the system supports up to 14 stops of dynamic range with an ARRI Cinema Mode recording at up to 4K60.

**Why it matters on this beat.** This is a device OEM naming its own imaging silicon — separate from the licensed application processor — and anchoring the resulting pipeline to a named cinema-industry partner's colour science, rather than describing camera quality only in megapixels.

[Source](https://www.honor.com/global/news/honor-magic9-launch/) · 28 September 2026

### vivo's X Fold6 pairs a MediaTek co-developed chip with vivo's own imaging silicon and an on-device meeting assistant
**vivo** · camera/imaging silicon, on-device AI · 30 September 2026 · Announced (global debut)

vivo's X Fold6 debuted globally on a "Dimensity 9500 Super Edition flagship platform, co-developed and highly optimized by vivo and MediaTek exclusively for X Fold6," paired with a separate "vivo Imaging Chip V3+" that vivo says "optimizes image processing to deliver sharper images, reduced noise, and improved power efficiency." The camera system combines a 200MP ZEISS main camera (Samsung HPB sensor) with a ZEISS APO periscope telephoto (Sony LYTIA 602 sensor, 3x optical, up to 100x "ZEISS HyperZoom"). The new OriginOS 7 Fold, run by what vivo calls its "Origin Smooth Engine," introduces a "Jovi" AI agent and an AI Meeting Assistant that vivo says captures in-person meetings "with speaker transcription and structured summaries" via an on-device AI Recorder.

**Why it matters on this beat.** MediaTek naming a chip variant as exclusively co-developed with one OEM is a different relationship than licensing an off-the-shelf flagship SoC, and it lands the same week a rival OEM (Honor) made the identical architectural choice — a dedicated ISP chip alongside the main AP — on Qualcomm silicon instead.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vivo-x-fold6-makes-global-debut-a-new-foldable-powerhouse-for-mobile-productivity-302894068.html) · 30 September 2026

## Standards and regulation

- Connecticut's Public Act 26-64 took effect 1 October 2026, requiring any business using facial recognition on its premises for security or fraud prevention to match faces only against a database it maintains itself, post "clearly legible signage" at each public entrance with a QR code or hyperlink to its facial-recognition policy, and include the Connecticut Attorney General's contact information in that policy. The Attorney General's office, which announced the requirement in advance, frames it as a consumer-notice measure rather than a ban. [source](https://portal.ct.gov/ag/press-releases/2026-press-releases/rights-and-requirements-related-to-new-and-updated-privacy-laws) (announced 16 September 2026; effective 1 October 2026)
