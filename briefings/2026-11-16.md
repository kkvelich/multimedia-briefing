# Multimedia & On-Device AI — week of 9 November to 15 November 2026

*The only item on this beat with real specifics this week came from NVIDIA, at a robotics conference — not from any of the nine principals' usual camera or multimedia-silicon channels, six of which published nothing in scope at all.*

## Top items

### NVIDIA ships Isaac Lab 3.0 and introduces VABAR, a robot-manipulation benchmark, at CoRL
**NVIDIA** · robotics vision · 9 November 2026 · shipped (Isaac Lab 3.0) / announced (VABAR)

At the Conference on Robot Learning, NVIDIA released Isaac Lab 3.0, its open-source robot-learning framework, adding a switchable PhysX/Newton physics solver and perception-based training renderers for vision-driven manipulation tasks. NVIDIA says the update delivers "up to 2.5x the training throughput of Isaac Lab 2.3" on a vision-based dexterous-manipulation benchmark — NVIDIA's own figure, not independently verified. Alongside it, NVIDIA and UC Berkeley released VABAR, a six-task benchmark for industrial robot manipulation, with thirteen partners named as building on the framework, including Bosch Research, Siemens and Skild AI.

**Why it matters on this beat.** Robot vision is one of this beat's form factors, and a quantified throughput figure for training vision-based manipulation policies — rather than a generic model benchmark — is a concrete claim about where and how fast perception models for robotics get trained, not just how large language models do.

[Source](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/robotics-simulation-advancements-corl/) · 9 November 2026

Arm names no camera or imaging use case for the core. [source](https://newsroom.arm.com/news/arm-cortex-m42) (10 November 2026)

[source](https://otter.ai/blog/otter-ai-announces-its-biggest-multilingual-leap-yet-27-new-languages-and-multilingual-capabilities) (10 November 2026)
